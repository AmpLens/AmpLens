Input notation

For massless four-, five- and six-point expressions.

Brackets

ab(i,j)$\langle i\,j\rangle$ sb(i,j)$[\,i\,j\,]$

Mandelstam invariants

s(i,j,…)$s_{ij\ldots}=(p_i+p_j+\ldots)^2$ s(i,j)$s_{ij}=\langle i\,j\rangle[\,j\,i\,]$

Dirac chains

chain('type', a, […], …, b)$\langle a|\cdots|b]$

Use a quoted type: 'aa', 'as', 'sa' or 'ss'. The two letters specify the left and right brackets, with a for angle and s for square. Each list specifies a sum of momenta.

chain('as',5,[1,6],2)$\langle 5|1{+}6|2]=\langle 5\,1\rangle[1\,2]+\langle 5\,6\rangle[6\,2]$
